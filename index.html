About Me

Welcome!

I joined Kuaishou in Feb. 2017, and currently I lead the video algorithm center there. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Peking University in 2014, where I was fortunate to be advised by Zongming Guo. I have visited National Tsing Hua University in 2023, where I was advised by Chia-wen Lin . My research interests include network optimization, video streaming, video encoding & processing, and video quality assessment. To date, I have published over 50 papers and hold more than 100 granted patents.

Short Bio:
I hold a Ph.D. from Peking University (2014) and began my career as a Senior Engineer at Huawei’s 2012 Lab, where I pioneered adaptive streaming technologies such as DASH and HLS. From 2015 to 2017, I served as a Research Scientist at two technology startups, focusing on the advancement of architectures and optimizations for real-time communication (RTC) and live streaming systems. Since joining Kuaishou in 2017, I have led large-scale video experience optimization initiatives through research and implementation in areas including network optimization, adaptive streaming systems, video encoding and processing, as well as video quality assessment. Currently, I am leading the video algorithm center at Kuaishou.
